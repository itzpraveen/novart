Project Story

Rahul Tirur

La 'Mansion is a masterclass in urban infill design, proving that a limited 4-cent plot is no barrier to luxury and scale. This contemporary residence maximizes interior volume through a tiered G+3 layout, defined by bold horizontal planes and striking vertical light features that create a prestigious street presence. Large-format glazing invites the surrounding tropical landscape inward, while the minimalist palette of crisp white and textured grey concrete ensures La 'Mansion maintains a timeless, sophisticated character.
