Project Story

HAMZA Venniyoor

Fathima's is a sophisticated transformation that breathes new life into a traditional structure, reimagining it as a minimalist tropical sanctuary. The design is defined by clean horizontal lines and a crisp white facade, beautifully contrasted by warm wood-cladding and integrated architectural lighting. From the serene outdoor pool with its textured stone feature wall to the expansive, double-height interiors featuring polished marble floors and bespoke fluted paneling, every detail reflects a seamless blend of luxury and functional simplicity.
