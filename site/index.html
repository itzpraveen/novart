Process

From site to handover

A clear sequence keeps the brief, drawings, approvals, materials, and site execution moving together.

Design intent stays visible through measurements, approvals, drawings, procurement, and site coordination.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We start with the site, your goals, and the daily life or work patterns the space needs to support.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Concepts are translated into planning, form, material direction, and interior atmosphere.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Drawings, details, coordination, and approvals are refined into a buildable system.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Execution is monitored closely so the finished space retains its intended clarity and character.
